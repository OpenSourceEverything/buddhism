The Noble Eightfold Path

  1. Right view: Understand suffering, its cause, ending, and path.
    • Mundane: Understand karma: actions have consequences across lives.
    • Supramundane: Directly understand the Four Noble Truths.
  2. Right intention: Intend renunciation, goodwill, and harmlessness.
  3. Right speech: Avoid lying, division, abuse, and idle chatter.
  4. Right action: Avoid killing, stealing, and sexual misconduct.
  5. Right livelihood: Earn without causing harm.
  6. Right effort: Prevent and abandon unskillful states; develop skillful ones.
  7. Right mindfulness: Clearly observe body, feeling, mind, and phenomena.
  8. Right concentration: Develop a unified mind through the four jhānas.

The path is one training with eight mutually supporting factors.
