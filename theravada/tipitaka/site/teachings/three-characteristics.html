Teachings

Three characteristics

Conditioned things are impermanent and unsatisfactory; all phenomena are not-self.

  • Impermanence: conditioned things arise, change, and cease.
  • Unsatisfactoriness: what changes cannot provide lasting security.
  • Not-self: no phenomenon can rightly be taken as a permanent self or possession.

Seeing these characteristics directly leads to disenchantment, dispassion, and release.
