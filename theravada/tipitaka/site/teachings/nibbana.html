Nibbāna

Nibbāna is the ending of greed, hatred, and delusion: release from suffering and rebirth.

Nibbāna is unconditioned and is realized through the cessation of craving. It can be directly known in this life.

With the death of an arahant, no condition remains for renewed existence.

Relevant suttas

  • Iti 44
