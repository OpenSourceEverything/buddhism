Practice

Four foundations of mindfulness

Observe body, feeling, mind, and phenomena clearly and without clinging.

  1. Body as body.
  2. Feeling as feeling.
  3. Mind as mind.
  4. Phenomena as phenomena.

Practice is ardent, clearly comprehending, and mindful, having put away craving and distress regarding the world.
