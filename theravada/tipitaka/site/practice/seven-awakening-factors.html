Seven awakening factors

Mindfulness, investigation, energy, rapture, tranquility, concentration, and equanimity support awakening.

  1. Mindfulness.
  2. Investigation of phenomena.
  3. Energy.
  4. Rapture.
  5. Tranquility.
  6. Concentration.
  7. Equanimity.

They are developed in dependence on seclusion, dispassion, and cessation, culminating in relinquishment.
