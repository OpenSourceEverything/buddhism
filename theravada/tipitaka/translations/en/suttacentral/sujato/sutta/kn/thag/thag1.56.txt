Verses of the Senior Monks 1.56
The Book of the Ones
Chapter Six
Kuṭivihārin (1st)
“Who is in this hut?” “A monk is in this hut,
free of lust, his mind serene.
Reverend, you should know this:
your hut wasn’t built in vain.”
