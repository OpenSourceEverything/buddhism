Verses of the Senior Monks 1.50
The Book of the Ones
Chapter Five
Vimala (1st)
The celestial lake Dharaṇī pours, the heavenly gale blows,
and lightning flashes across the sky!
But my thoughts are stilled,
my mind is serene.
