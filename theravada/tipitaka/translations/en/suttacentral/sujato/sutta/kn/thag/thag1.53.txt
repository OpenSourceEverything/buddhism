Verses of the Senior Monks 1.53
The Book of the Ones
Chapter Six
Valliya (1st)
The heavens rain like a sweet song;
my hut is roofed and pleasant, sheltered from the wind.
I meditate there, diligent:
so rain forth, heavens, if you wish.
