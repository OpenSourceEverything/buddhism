Verses of the Senior Monks 2.7
The Book of the Twos
Chapter One
Rādha
Just as rain seeps into
a poorly roofed house,
lust seeps into
an undeveloped mind.
Just as rain doesn’t seep into
a well roofed house,
lust doesn’t seep into
a well-developed mind.
