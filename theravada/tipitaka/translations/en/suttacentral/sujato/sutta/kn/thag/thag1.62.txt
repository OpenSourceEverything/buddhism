Verses of the Senior Monks 1.62
The Book of the Ones
Chapter Seven
Vajjiputta (1st)
We dwell alone in the wilderness,
like a log dumped in a forest.
Lots of people are jealous of me,
as beings in hell are of one going to heaven.
