So It Was Said 51
The Book of the Threes
Chapter One
Elements
This was said by the Buddha, the Perfected One: that is what I heard.
“Mendicants, there are these three elements. What three? The realm of luminous form, the formless realm, and cessation. These are the three elements.”
The Buddha spoke this matter. On this it is said:
“Comprehending the realm of luminous form,
not stuck in the formless,
those who are released in cessation,
they are conquerors of death.
Having directly experienced the element free of death,
free of attachments;
having realized relinquishment
of attachments, the undefiled
fully awakened Buddha teaches
the sorrowless, stainless state.”
This too is a matter that was spoken by the Blessed One: that is what I heard.
