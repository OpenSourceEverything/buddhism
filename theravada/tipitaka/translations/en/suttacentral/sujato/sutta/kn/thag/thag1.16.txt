Verses of the Senior Monks 1.16
The Book of the Ones
Chapter Two
Belaṭṭhasīsa
Just as a fine thoroughbred
proceeds with ease,
tail and mane flying in the wind;
so my days and nights
proceed with ease,
full of joy not of the flesh.
