Numbered Discourses 10.132
13. Purified
Tenth
“Mendicants, there are ten wrong ways. What ten? Wrong view, wrong purpose, wrong speech, wrong action, wrong livelihood, wrong effort, wrong mindfulness, wrong immersion, wrong knowledge, and wrong freedom. These are the ten wrong ways.”
