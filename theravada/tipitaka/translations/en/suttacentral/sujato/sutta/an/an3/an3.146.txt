Numbered Discourses 3.146
15. Good Fortune
Unskillful
“Someone with three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. What three? Unskillful deeds by way of body, speech, and mind.
Someone with these three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Someone with three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. What three? Skillful deeds by way of body, speech, and mind.
Someone with these three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
