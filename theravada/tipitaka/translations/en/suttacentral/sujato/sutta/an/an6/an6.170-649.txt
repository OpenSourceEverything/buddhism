Numbered Discourses 6.170–649
13. Abbreviated Texts Beginning with Greed
Untitled Discourses on Hate, Etc.
“For insight into hate … delusion … anger … acrimony … disdain … contempt … jealousy … stinginess … deceitfulness … deviousness … obstinacy … aggression … conceit … arrogance … vanity …
For insight into negligence … complete understanding … complete ending … giving up … ending … vanishing … fading away … cessation … giving away … For the letting go of negligence these six things should be developed.”
That is what the Buddha said. Satisfied, the mendicants approved what the Buddha said.
The Book of the Sixes is finished.
