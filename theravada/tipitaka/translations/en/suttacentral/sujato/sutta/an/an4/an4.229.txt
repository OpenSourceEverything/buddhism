Numbered Discourses 4.229
23. Bad Conduct
Lack of Conscience
“A foolish person … brims with much wickedness. … They’re faithless, unethical, shameless, and imprudent. … An astute person … brims with much merit. … They’re faithful, ethical, conscientious, and prudent. …”
