Numbered Discourses 10.77
8. If You Want
A Crow
“Mendicants, a crow possesses ten bad qualities. What ten? They’re rude and impudent, gluttonous and voracious, cruel and pitiless, weak and raucous, unmindful and acquisitive. A crow possesses these ten bad qualities. In the same way, a bad mendicant possesses these ten bad qualities. What ten? They’re rude and impudent, gluttonous and voracious, cruel and pitiless, weak and raucous, unmindful and acquisitive. A bad mendicant possesses these ten bad qualities.”
