Linked Discourses 22.32
3. The Burden
Brittle
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, I will teach you what is brittle and what is not brittle. Listen …
And what is brittle? What is not brittle? Form is brittle, but its cessation, settling, and disappearance is not brittle.
Feeling … perception … choices … consciousness is brittle, but its cessation, settling, and disappearance is not brittle.”
