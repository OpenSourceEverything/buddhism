Linked Discourses 55.50
5. Overflowing Merit, With Verses
Factors
“Mendicants, there are these four factors for stream-entry. What four? Associating with true persons, listening to the true teaching, rational application of mind, and practicing in line with the teaching. These are the four factors for stream-entry.”
