Linked Discourses 22.147
14. Burning Chaff
Observing Impermanence
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, when a gentleman has gone forth out of faith, this is what’s in line with the teaching. They should meditate observing impermanence in form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness. … They’re freed from suffering, I say.”
