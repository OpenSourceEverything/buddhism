Linked Discourses 32.1
1. Gods of the Thunderclouds
Plain Version
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, I will teach you about the gods of the thundercloud host. Listen …
And what are the gods of the thundercloud host? There are gods of the cool thunderclouds, warm thunderclouds, stormy thunderclouds, windy thunderclouds, and rainy thunderclouds. These are called gods of the thundercloud host.”
