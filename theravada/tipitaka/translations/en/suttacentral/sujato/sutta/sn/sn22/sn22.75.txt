Linked Discourses 22.75
8. Bitable
Origin (2nd)
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, a learned noble disciple truly understands the origin, the disappearance, the gratification, the drawback, and the escape when it comes to form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness.”
