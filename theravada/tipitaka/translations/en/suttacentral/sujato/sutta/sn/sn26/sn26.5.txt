Linked Discourses 26.5
1. Arising
Feeling
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, the arising of feeling born of eye contact …
the arising of feeling born of mind contact … is the manifestation of old age and death. The cessation of feeling born of eye contact … the cessation of feeling born of mind contact … is the disappearance of old age and death.”
