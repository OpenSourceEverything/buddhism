Linked Discourses 26.3
1. Arising
Consciousness
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, the arising of eye consciousness … mind consciousness … is the manifestation of old age and death. The cessation of eye consciousness … mind consciousness … is the disappearance of old age and death.”
