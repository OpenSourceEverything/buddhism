Linked Discourses 23.22
2. About Māra (2nd)
Liable to Cease
At Sāvatthī.
Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“Sir, they speak of a ‘liability to cease’. What has a liability to cease?”
“Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness have a liability to cease.
Seeing this … They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
