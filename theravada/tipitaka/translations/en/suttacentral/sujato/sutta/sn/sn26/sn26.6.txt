Linked Discourses 26.6
1. Arising
Perception
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, the arising of perception of sights … perception of ideas … is the manifestation of old age and death. The cessation of perception of sights … perception of ideas … is the disappearance of old age and death.”
