Linked Discourses 26.8
1. Arising
Craving
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, the arising of craving for sights … craving for ideas … is the manifestation of old age and death. The cessation of craving for sights … craving for ideas … is the disappearance of old age and death.”
