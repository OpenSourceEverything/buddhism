Linked Discourses 45.38
4. Practice
The Brahmin Life (2nd)
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, I will teach you life as a brahmin and the goal of life as a brahmin. Listen …
And what is life as a brahmin? It is simply this noble eightfold path, that is: right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right immersion. This is called life as a brahmin.
And what is the goal of life as a brahmin? The ending of greed, hate, and delusion. This is called the goal of life as a brahmin.”
