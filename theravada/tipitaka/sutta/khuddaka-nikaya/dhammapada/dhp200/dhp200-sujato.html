Dhp 200
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp200/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Let us live so very happily,
we who have nothing.
We shall feed on rapture,
like the gods of sublime luminosity.
