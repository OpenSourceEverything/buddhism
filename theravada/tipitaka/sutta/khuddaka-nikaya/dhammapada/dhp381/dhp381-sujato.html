Dhp 381
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp381/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

A monk full of joy
trusting in the Buddha’s teaching,
would realize the peaceful state,
the blissful stilling of conditions.
