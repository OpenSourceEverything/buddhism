Dhp 349
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp349/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

For a personage churned by thoughts,
very lustful, focusing on beauty,
their craving grows and grows,
tying them with a stout bond.
