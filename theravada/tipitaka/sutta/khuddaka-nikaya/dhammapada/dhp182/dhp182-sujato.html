Dhp 182
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp182/en/sujato

It’s hard to gain a human birth;
the life of mortals is hard;
it’s hard to hear the true teaching;
the arising of Buddhas is hard.
