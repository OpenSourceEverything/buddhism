Dhp 356
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp356/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Weeds are the bane of crops,
but greed is these folk’s bane.
That’s why a gift to one rid of greed
is so very fruitful.
