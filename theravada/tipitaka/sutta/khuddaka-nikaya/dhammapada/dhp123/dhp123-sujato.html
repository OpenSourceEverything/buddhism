Dhp 123
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp123/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Avoid wickedness,
as a merchant with rich cargo and small escort
would avoid a dangerous road,
or one who loves life would avoid drinking poison.
