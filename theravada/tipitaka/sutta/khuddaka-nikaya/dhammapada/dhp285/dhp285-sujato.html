Dhp 285
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp285/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Cut out affection for oneself,
like plucking an autumn lotus.
Foster only the path to peace,
the extinguishment the Holy One taught.
