Dhp 72
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp72/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Whatever fame a fool may get,
it only gives rise to harm.
Whatever good features they have it ruins,
and blows their head into bits.
