Dhp 369
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp369/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Bail out this boat, mendicant!
When bailed out it will float lightly.
Having cut off desire and hate,
you shall reach extinguishment.
