Dhp 50
Translated by Bhikkhu Sujato
Source: https://suttacentral.net/dhp50/en/sujato
License: CC0-1.0

Don’t find fault with others,
with what they’ve done or left undone.
You should only watch yourself,
what you’ve done or left undone.
