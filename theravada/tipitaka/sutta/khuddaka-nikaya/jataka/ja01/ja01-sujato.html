Stories of Past Lives 1
The Book of the Ones
Sure Bet
Sure Bet: a past life story
Homage to him, the blessed one, the perfected one, the fully awakened Buddha!
Some stand on a sure bet,
while sophists speak of a second way.
An intelligent person, knowing this,
would take up the sure bet.
