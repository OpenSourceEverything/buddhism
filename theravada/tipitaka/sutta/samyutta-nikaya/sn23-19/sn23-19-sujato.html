Linked Discourses 23.19
2. About Māra (2nd)
Liable to End
At Sāvatthī.
Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“Sir, they speak of a ‘liability to end’. What has a liability to end?”
“Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness have a liability to end.
Seeing this … They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
