Linked Discourses 29.5
1. Dragons
Sabbath (3rd)
At Sāvatthī.
Seated to one side, that mendicant said to the Buddha:
“Sir, what is the cause, what is the reason why some moisture-born dragons keep the sabbath, having transformed their bodies?”
(Tell all in full.)
