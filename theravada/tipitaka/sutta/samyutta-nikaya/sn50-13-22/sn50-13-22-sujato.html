Linked Discourses 50.13–22
2. Diligence
Diligence
(Tell in full as in the chapter on diligence at SN 45.139–148.)
The Realized One, footprint, roof peak,
roots, heartwood, jasmine,
monarch, sun and moon,
and cloth is the tenth.
