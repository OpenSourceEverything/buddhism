Linked Discourses 23.18
2. About Māra (2nd)
Naturally Not-Self
At Sāvatthī.
Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“Sir, they speak of having ‘not-self nature’. What has a nature of not-self?”
“Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness have a nature of not-self.
Seeing this … They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
