Linked Discourses 22.41
4. It’s Not Yours
In Line With the Teaching (3rd)
At Sāvatthī.
“Mendicants, when a mendicant is practicing in line with the teaching, this is what’s in line with the teaching. They should meditate observing suffering in form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness. … They’re freed from suffering, I say.”
