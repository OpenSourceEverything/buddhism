Linked Discourses 23.20
2. About Māra (2nd)
Liable to Fall
At Sāvatthī.
Seated to one side, Venerable Rādha said to the Buddha:
“Sir, they speak of a ‘liability to fall’. What has a liability to fall?”
“Rādha, form, feeling, perception, choices, and consciousness have a liability to fall.
Seeing this … They understand: ‘… there is nothing further for this place.’”
