Linked Discourses 55.48
5. Overflowing Merit, With Verses
With Bhaddiya
At Kapilavatthu. Seated to one side, the Buddha said to Bhaddiya the Sakyan:
“Bhaddiya, a noble disciple who has four things is a stream-enterer …”
