Numbered Discourses 7.93
9. An Ascetic
Bad Qualities
“Mendicants, there are these seven bad qualities. What seven? Someone is faithless, shameless, imprudent, unlearned, lazy, unmindful, and witless. These are the seven bad qualities.”
