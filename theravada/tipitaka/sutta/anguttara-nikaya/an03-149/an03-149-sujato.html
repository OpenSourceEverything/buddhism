Numbered Discourses 3.149
15. Good Fortune
Impure
“Someone with three qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. … Impure deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …
Someone with three qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. … Pure deeds by way of body, speech, and mind. …”
