347
“… so too the sentient beings who get the taste of the meaning, the taste of the teaching, and the taste of freedom are few, while the sentient beings who don’t get the taste of the meaning, the taste of the teaching, and the taste of freedom are many.
So you should train like this: ‘We will get the taste of the meaning, the taste of the teaching, the taste of freedom.’ That’s how you should train.”
