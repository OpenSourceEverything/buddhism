Numbered Discourses 2.180–229
The Chapter of Abbreviated Texts Beginning With Anger
180–184
“There are these two things. What two? Anger and acrimony … disdain and contempt … jealousy and stinginess … deceit and deviousness … lack of conscience and prudence. These are the two things.”
