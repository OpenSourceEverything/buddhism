Numbered Discourses 4.273
27. Ways of Performing Deeds
Wrong View
“… They themselves have wrong view …
They themselves have right view; they encourage others to have right view; they approve of right view; and they praise right view. Someone with these four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
