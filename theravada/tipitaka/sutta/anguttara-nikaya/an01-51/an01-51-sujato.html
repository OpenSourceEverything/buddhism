Numbered Discourses 1.51–60
The Chapter on a Finger-Snap
51
“This mind, mendicants, is radiant. But it is corrupted by visiting corruptions. An unlearned ordinary person does not truly understand this. So I say that the unlearned ordinary person has no development of the mind.”
