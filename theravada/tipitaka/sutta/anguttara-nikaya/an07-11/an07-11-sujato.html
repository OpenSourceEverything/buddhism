Numbered Discourses 7.11
2. Tendencies
Underlying Tendencies (1st)
“Mendicants, there are these seven underlying tendencies. What seven? The underlying tendencies of sensual desire, aversion, views, doubt, conceit, desire for continued existence, and ignorance. These are the seven underlying tendencies.”
