Numbered Discourses 10.125
13. Purified
Third
“Mendicants, these ten things are not very fruitful and beneficial apart from the Holy One’s training. What ten? Right view, right purpose, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, right immersion, right knowledge, and right freedom. These are the ten things that are not very fruitful and beneficial apart from the Holy One’s training.”
