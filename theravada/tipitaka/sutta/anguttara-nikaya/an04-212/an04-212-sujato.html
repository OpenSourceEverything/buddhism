Numbered Discourses 4.212
22. Assembly
View
“Mendicants, someone with four qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there. What four? Bad conduct by way of body, speech, and mind, and wrong view. Someone with these four qualities is placed in hell as if delivered there.
Someone with four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there. What four? Good conduct by way of body, speech, and mind, and right view. Someone with these four qualities is placed in heaven as if delivered there.”
