Numbered Discourses 5.101
11. Living Comfortably
Assurance
“Mendicants, these five qualities make a trainee self-assured. What five? It’s when a mendicant is faithful, ethical, learned, energetic, and wise.
A person of faith doesn’t have the insecurities of someone who lacks faith. So this quality makes a trainee self-assured.
An ethical person doesn’t have the insecurities of someone who is unethical. So this quality makes a trainee self-assured.
A learned person doesn’t have the insecurities of a person of little learning. So this quality makes a trainee self-assured.
An energetic person doesn’t have the insecurities of a lazy person. So this quality makes a trainee self-assured.
A wise person doesn’t have the insecurities of someone who is witless. So this quality makes a trainee self-assured.
These are the five qualities that make a trainee self-assured.”
